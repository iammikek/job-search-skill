## Status words

`Idea` · `Researching` · `Outreach drafted` · `Sent` · `Applied` · `Replied` · `In process` · `Follow-up due` · `Paused` · `Closed` · `Rejected fit`

Silence of 7 days or more after a send on a live thread moves the row to `Follow-up due`. After one short bump and further silence, move it to `Paused` unless the owner says otherwise.

`Rejected fit` is the owner's skip. `Rejected` in the volume log is an employer decline of a seat that was applied to. Do not mix them.
