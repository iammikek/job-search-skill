# Volume

The week log is the source of truth. Totals are the sum of its columns. Do not keep a second running total.

Week = Monday to Sunday in the owner's timezone. The start date is the `started` line on the index.

If today's Monday has no row, append one with zeros and mark it live. Increment that row. Do not rewrite a closed week unless correcting a named miss.

| Week commencing | Seen | Screened | Applied | Rejected | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD Live. | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | |

## Totals

Seen 0. Screened 0. Applied 0. Rejected 0.

## What increments

| Counter | Increment when | Do not increment |
|---|---|---|
| Seen | A named seat (company plus role, or an application id) is first written down | The same seat seen again on another board. A board's total count of jobs |
| Screened | That seat first gets numeric scores | A rescore. A row with no scores |
| Applied | The owner submits an application on a named seat | A speculative note, a connection request, a recruiter registration, a keep-warm message |
| Rejected | An applied seat gets an employer decline, or the role closes after an application without an interview | A seat the owner skipped. Silence. A pause. The owner walking away |

Name the seat in the week-log notes. Recompute the totals from the week log after every increment.
