## Constraints

- **draft-only** · Applications and replies are drafted here. The agent does not send email, a board form, or a message.
- **channel** · Name the channels that do not get replies. A weekly send uses a channel that does.
- **excludes** · Name the employers that stay off the shortlist.
- **hard-reject** · Name the scores that fail a seat after it has been scored. Suggested start: C4 = 1 or C5 = 1 fails unless the owner overrides in writing. C3 = 1 is a hard miss.
- **discovery-wide** · Do not drop a title before scoring.
- **freshness** · Set the maximum age of a new posting. Suggested start: two calendar months. No date means do not apply until the date is confirmed.
- **silent-apply** · Set how long a silent application waits before it is paused. Suggested start: about one month. A pause is not an employer decline.
- **review-due** · A measurement whose review date is today or earlier is not in force. Stop. Name the line. Ask the owner to keep it or delete it. Do not invent the next phase.
- **in-flight** · Before editing the pipeline, the volume log, the week strategy, or a weekly pass, check that those files are clean and that no other pass is open on a branch. If either is true, do not edit the files another session owns.

## Measurements

Re-read on the review date, or delete the line. Constraints above do not expire.

- **cash** · Set the weekly pass floor, the figure to negotiate toward, and the band to accept faster. A stated cap under the floor is a hard skip. Do not name the floor in an application.
- **volume-cap** · Set how many sends are allowed in a Monday week.
- **stack** · Name the stacks that score high on C4.
- **place** · Name what remote, local, and commute mean. Score the journey. Do not skip a seat on distance alone unless this line says so.
- **shape** · Name the seat shape to prefer. Score management load on C3 and seniority on C7. They are different.
- **open-title** · Say whether a missing seniority word keeps a seat off the shortlist. Suggested start: it does not. Score C7 honestly.
- **blank-pay** · Say whether a missing salary blocks an application. Suggested start: it does not. Score C1 as 3 and ask when a person replies.
