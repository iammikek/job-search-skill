# Example rubric

A fictional owner. The numbers, stacks, and employers are examples. Copy the shape. Use them in the private book only when the owner chooses those answers.

## What they told the wizard

1. Cash. Pass floor £50,000. Negotiate toward £95,000. Accept £72,000 to £78,000 faster when place and tenure are clear. Contract work is in the hunt: 220 billable days, perm-equivalent factor 0.75, shortest term six months.
2. Work. Go and TypeScript score high. Remote in the UK, or a Manchester office, is the place they want. A longer journey scores lower on C2. It is not a skip.
3. Seat. Prefer hands-on individual contributor. People management is C3 = 1. A missing "Senior" in the title does not keep a seat off the shortlist.
4. Fit. Prefer climate and education products. Down-favour advertising technology and gambling. Leave Northwind Systems off the shortlist.
5. Pace. Four sends in a Monday week. A new posting older than two calendar months is not a new application. Silence of about one month pauses an application. Week timezone Europe/London. Volume log starts Monday 2026-01-05.
