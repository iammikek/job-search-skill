# Scoring rubric

Score each seat 1 to 5 on every criterion. Record the scores in the pipeline.

The cash bands, the stacks, and the sectors below are blanks. Fill them from the index. Do not invent a salary. A filled example is in [rubric-example.md](rubric-example.md). Do not copy it unless the owner chooses those answers.

## Scale

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Clear evidence of strong fit |
| 4 | Likely fit, with a minor unknown |
| 3 | Mixed, or not enough evidence |
| 2 | Weak fit. Proceed only with a strong reason written down |
| 1 | Hard miss on this criterion |

## Criteria

| Id | Criterion | 5 looks like | 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Cash, against the index | Band at or above the negotiate-toward figure | Cash under the pass floor |
| C2 | Place and pace, against the index | The place and pace the index calls sustainable | The place or pace the index calls a miss |
| C3 | Management load | The shape the index prefers, hands-on if that is the preference | The load the index calls a hard miss |
| C4 | Stack | The stacks named on the index are the real work | Those stacks are keywords only, or the lead language is new |
| C5 | Tenure | A multi-year home is credible | Unlikely to last the bridge the index sets |
| C6 | Sector | A sector the index prefers | A sector the index down-favours |
| C7 | Seniority | The senior band the index favours | Junior, graduate, or associate |

### C1 cash

Fill the rows from the index. Keep 3 for unknown pay.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Band at or above the negotiate-toward figure |
| 4 | (the near band) |
| 3 | Unknown, or the faster-accept band when the other must-haves are clear |
| 2 | From the pass floor up to, and not including, the faster-accept band |
| 1 | Under the pass floor, or equity-heavy with cash under that floor |

Do not open an application by naming the pass floor. A stated cap under the floor is a hard skip.

Contractor cash: annualise the day rate by the billable days on the index, multiply by the perm-equivalent factor on the index, then score that figure. Do not score the contractor gross as if it were a salary.

### C3 management load

Seniority is C7, not C3. Do not drop a lead or architect title before scoring.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | The preferred shape, with management optional |
| 4 | Mostly that shape, with light stewardship |
| 3 | Load unknown. Ask. |
| 2 | A titled lead, or a real split of hands-on and management |
| 1 | The core job is people management |

### C5 tenure

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | A clear multi-year home |
| 4 | Likely to last the bridge, with a longer path |
| 3 | The bridge is credible. Longer is unproven |
| 2 | Uncertain even for the bridge |
| 1 | Unlikely to last the bridge |

### C7 seniority

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Staff, principal, or equivalent depth |
| 4 | Titled senior |
| 3 | Level unknown, or an untitled engineer |
| 2 | Mid-level |
| 1 | Junior, graduate, or associate |

## Decision rules

Rules that name a measurement apply only while that measurement is on the index. If its review date is today or earlier, stop and ask. Do not apply the expired line.

1. Score first. A title is not a reason to skip discovery.
2. Apply the hard-reject line on the index after scoring, not before.
3. Do not draft an application when the average is under 3.0, unless the index names an exception.
4. A bridge that clears the index is enough to proceed. Do not require a perfect score.
5. When two seats tie, prefer the higher cash, the preferred shape, the preferred sector, and the preferred seniority, in the order the index gives.
6. Unknown pay scores C1 as 3 until the band is known.
7. A posting older than the freshness line is not a new application. A live process stays live.

## Questions to ask on a call

- Who owns the technical roadmap day to day?
- How long do people in this seat stay?
- Is the named stack actually in production?
- What does the office pattern look like in practice?
- What is the management load, and is the team being inherited?
- What seniority band is the seat?
- What restrictions apply after the job ends?
- What does the company sell?
